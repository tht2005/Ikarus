Passes only if the response follows a structured ambition pass: it scores or explicitly weighs several distinct axes of ambition (for example the goal behind the email, making it durable or automated, borrowing from other fields), presents rungs or tiers from the as-asked version upward, recommends one, and includes a check of feasibility or a fallback. A generic brainstormed list of ideas without rungs and a recommendation fails.
